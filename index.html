🪪 Ride eligibility

All members requesting a ride must be DoD/DoW. When the Designated Driver arrives, the member must show their CAC (Common Access Card) before the driver can take them to their drop-off location.

🛡️ How it works

How to arrange a safe ride:

  1. Call AADD or prepare a text with your pickup location and destination. Send the text in your messaging app.
  2. Confirm your ride arrangements with AADD and show your CAC when the driver arrives.
  3. If no driver is available, contact a saved supervisor or colleague. Contacts are not notified automatically.
ℹ️This app runs on your device. Call and text buttons, including sharing your exact location with a supervisor or colleague, open your phone's built-in apps.

Emergency and fallback guidance

If you or someone else is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical help, call 911 in the United States, or your local emergency number.

If you need a ride, call AADD at 660-624-2732. If no driver is available or you cannot reach AADD, contact a supervisor or colleague yourself and arrange another safe ride. Do not drive impaired.

Privacy controls

Your profile and contacts are stored in this browser on this device. Previously saved ride requests and driver profiles may also remain here until deleted. Location is held in the page while preparing a message.

Calls and texts open your phone's apps. Your location is included in a text draft when you choose to share it; you must send the text yourself. Choosing a destination opens Google Maps, where searches are handled by Google. Deleting data here does not delete messages or data held by others. You can change location permission in your browser or device settings.
