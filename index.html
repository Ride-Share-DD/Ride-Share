🛡️ How it works

Three ways this keeps you safe:

  1. Request a ride and share your exact location. Sharing with a supervisor or colleague opens your own messaging app, so the text comes from your number.
  2. A nearby Designated Driver accepts and comes to get you.
  3. If no driver is available, your saved supervisors or colleagues are contacted one at a time in the order you set.
ℹ️This app runs on your device. Call and text buttons, including sharing your exact location with a supervisor or colleague, open your phone's built-in apps.
