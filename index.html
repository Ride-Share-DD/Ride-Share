🛡️ How it works

Three ways this keeps you safe:

  1. Request a ride and share your exact location.
  2. A nearby Designated Driver accepts and comes to get you.
  3. If no driver is available, your saved supervisors are contacted one at a time in the order you set.
ℹ️This app runs on your device. Call and text buttons open your phone's real dialer and messages app.
