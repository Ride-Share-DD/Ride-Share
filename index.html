🛡️ How it works

Three ways this keeps you safe:

  1. Request a ride and share your exact location.
  2. A nearby Designated Driver accepts and comes to get you.
  3. If no driver is available, your saved supervisors are contacted one at a time, in the order you set — a true last resort.
